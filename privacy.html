Privacy Protocol

Competentraders Data Protection 2026

01. Data Custodianship

We act as the primary controller of your data. By using Competentraders, you grant us the right to process information strictly necessary for investment execution and identity verification.

02. Identity Information

We collect your legal name, email, and telephone number. This data is used to prevent duplicate accounts and ensure the integrity of our referral network.

03. Financial Metadata

Wallet addresses and transaction hashes (TXIDs) are logged to track deposit success. We do not store your private keys or Opay login passwords.

04. Encryption Standards

User data is secured using AES-256 bit encryption at rest. All dashboard communications are transmitted via Secure Socket Layer (SSL) protocols.

05. Zero-Sale Policy

Competentraders Global will NEVER sell your email or contact history to third-party marketing brokers or external scammers.

06. KYC Compliance

To meet international Anti-Money Laundering (AML) standards, we may request government-issued identification to unlock high-volume withdrawal limits.

07. IP Intelligence

We log IP addresses to protect your account from "brute force" attacks. Rapid IP changes from high-risk VPNs may trigger an automatic security hold.

08. Session Cookies

We use essential cookies to maintain your login session. These expire automatically after 24 hours of inactivity for your protection.

09. Withdrawal Gateway Privacy

When you withdraw to Opay or Bank accounts, only the necessary account number is shared with our automated payout partner to facilitate the transfer.

10. Right to Erasure

You have the "Right to be Forgotten." Upon closing your account, your personal identifiers are scrubbed from our active servers within 60 days.

11. Behavioral Monitoring

We use anonymized behavioral data (clicks and navigation) to improve the dashboard UI and detect bot-assisted script exploits.

12. Next-of-Kin Data

Optional emergency contact data is stored securely and only accessed in the event of documented user incapacity or demise.

13. Email Notifications

System-critical emails (Password resets, Withdrawal alerts) cannot be opted out of, as they are vital for account security.

14. Law Enforcement

We will only disclose user data to authorities if presented with a valid judicial subpoena or court order from a recognized jurisdiction.

15. Data Breach Protocol

In the event of a security compromise, we will notify affected users via their registered email within 72 hours of discovery.

16. Support Chat Privacy

Conversations with our Chat Terminal are logged for quality control and to provide a history of your technical requests.

17. Cross-Border Transfers

Your data may be stored on decentralized cloud servers across multiple continents to ensure 99.9% uptime and redundancy.

18. No Biometric Storage

We do not store fingerprints or facial scans. Any "Face Verification" is handled by third-party ID partners with their own privacy tiers.

19. External Social Links

We are not responsible for the privacy practices of external Telegram or WhatsApp groups not explicitly managed by the Core Team.

20. Marketing Consent

By providing profit testimonials, you allow us to use your username and earnings amount for promotional graphics.

21. Minors

We do not knowingly collect data from individuals under 18.

22. Data Accuracy

Users are responsible for keeping their email and bank info updated.

23. Internal Access

Only senior security engineers have access to raw user database files.

24. Password Hashing

Passwords are salted and hashed; no staff member can see your plain text password.

25. Ledger Integrity

Public blockchain data (TXIDs) is inherently transparent and beyond our control.

26. Automated Decisions

Security bots may auto-freeze accounts if "brute force" patterns are detected.

27. Modification Notice

Privacy changes are posted here; continued use constitutes acceptance.

28. Contact DPO

Contact our Data Protection Officer at competentraders@gmail.com for inquiries.

29. Anti-Spam

We use honeypots to catch and block scrapers trying to steal user emails.

30. Final Affirmation

Your privacy is your power. We defend it as our own. Securely Lifting Lives.

This protocol is binding upon all global users. Amen.
