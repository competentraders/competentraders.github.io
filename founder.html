My Story

My name is **Ugbesia Godswill**. I am 30 years old. I studied Computer Science at Delta State Polytechnic, Ogwashiku. I am an engineer who works with both computer software and hardware. I also have many years of experience in Forex and Cryptocurrency trading.

But more than that, I am someone who cares. It pains my heart when I see people in the streets struggling with no help. It hurts to see people who are poor and have no way to make their lives better.

I know many people have made mistakes in the past, but I believe they need someone to forgive them, listen to them, and give them a **second chance**.

"I am a problem solver. I do not rest until a problem is fixed. I am using my skills to lift as many lives as I can through this website."

I cannot help every person in the world, and I will not tell you that you will become rich overnight. But I promise that this website can be a way for you to pay your bills and live a life that makes sense.

Note: We do not have a physical office building. All our services are done online so that we can reach everyone, everywhere.

To the glory of GOD, we will all succeed together. Amen.
