Account Architecture

What is Edge Storage Security?
Competentraders utilizes Edge Storage, meaning your sensitive data is encrypted and saved locally on your device. This eliminates central server hacks and ensures you are the only one with access to your private terminal.
Can I have multiple accounts?
System policy allows one primary account per device. Multiple accounts may trigger a security flag in our node, leading to a temporary hold on withdrawals.
Why can't I change my email?
For security reasons, your email is hardcoded as your unique identifier. If you must change it, you will need to undergo a full KYC verification with the CEO office.

Cash Flow & Settlements

Why only Monday Payouts?
We operate on a Weekly Settlement Cycle. This allows our traders to maximize market liquidity during the week without interrupting active trade flows. Mondays are dedicated entirely to capital disbursement.
What is the minimum withdrawal?
The minimum threshold for withdrawal is ₦2,000. This ensures we can process batch settlements efficiently through the Opay and Kuda banking nodes.
My deposit hasn't appeared yet?
Deposits usually take 5-30 minutes. If you have clicked "I Have Paid" and sent your receipt to the WhatsApp desk, rest assured your node is being activated manually.
Are there any hidden withdrawal fees?
We believe in 100% transparency. There are no hidden fees. The amount you see in your balance is exactly what will be sent to your bank, minus standard bank transfer charges (₦10-₦50).

Risk Management

What happens if a trade is lost?
Our systems use "Stop-Loss" protocols. While trading carries risk, our algorithm is designed to protect your initial capital first before seeking profit.
Is my bank information safe?
We never store your full bank login details. We only require your Account Number and Bank Name for the sole purpose of sending you your profits.

System Maintenance

Why does my balance show ₦0 after refresh?
Ensure you are using the same browser and device. Since data is saved locally, clearing your cache or using "Incognito Mode" will hide your balance. Log in again to restore view.
The site is loading slowly?
This usually occurs during "Monday Payouts" due to high traffic on our node. Refresh the page or check your internet connection for optimal terminal performance.
