Login & Registration

How do I register?
Click "Register" on the login page. Enter your details and set a strong password. Note that your data is saved locally on this device for your privacy.
I forgot my login password?
Since we use local storage for security, if you forget your password, you may need to clear your browser cache and register again. Contact support for recovery assistance.

Money & Transactions

How do I deposit money?
Go to the Deposit page, copy the Opay account details, make the transfer from your bank app, and click "I Have Paid." Send your receipt to competentraders@gmail.com.
When will I receive my withdrawal?
Withdrawal requests are reviewed and processed within 24 to 48 hours. Ensure your bank details are correctly saved in the "Bank Account" section.

User Profile

Can I change my profile name?
Yes, go to the "User Profile" page in the menu. Update your name and hit save; it will instantly update across the dashboard and referral pages.

Investment Plans

How do the Sports Plans work?
When you buy a Sports Plan, our experts trade for you using professional sports betting strategies. Your returns are added to your balance at the end of the duration.
Can I cancel an active plan?
Active investment plans cannot be canceled once started as the funds are already committed to live trades/strategies.

More & Security

Is my balance safe?
Your balance is stored on your device's secure local storage. To keep it 100% safe, do not share your phone or login details with anyone.
