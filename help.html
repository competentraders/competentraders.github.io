Getting Started

Follow these 4 steps to earn

1

Setup Your Profile

Go to the Profile page and enter your Full Name. This ensures your account and referral ID are correctly linked to you.

2

Fund Your Account

Visit the Deposit page. Transfer your desired amount to the Opay account provided and click "I Have Paid" to update your balance.

3

Choose a Strategy

Select between Sports Betting or Forex Investment plans on the dashboard. Click "Buy Subscription" to start earning.

4

Withdraw Profits

Once your investment cycle is complete, go to the Withdrawal page to send your earnings to your bank or crypto wallet.
